# Identity

Folio is an **editorial** system: it reads like a well-set publication.

- **Type:** DM Sans for the interface and prose; **Source Serif 4** for display (titles, entry titles, the brand), weight 600, tracking −0.01em.
- **Collection as an index:** entries grouped under small uppercase group titles, each with a cover (or initials), a serif title, a tagline in prose and a meta line; filters in their own column, sticky from 64rem.
- **Article:** an uppercase eyebrow path, a large serif title, a lead in the display face, a side column (panel and facts) sticky from 64rem, and a bounded figure **only when an image exists**.
- **Form:** a centered column with a serif title.
- **Editorial panel:** a deep green panel (`panel`) with light text (`on-panel`) and a pale green accent (`panel-accent`) for the eyebrow, the decorative ring and focus inside it.
- **Geometry and depth:** softer radii (8 px controls, 12 px cards, pill chips), a subtle card shadow in light mode only, 150 ms motion.
- **Brand:** the logo slot's text fallback is the product label in the serif face.

Consumers may change any configurable value; these rules describe the defaults.
